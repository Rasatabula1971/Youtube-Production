# Human Packaging Gate

## Purpose

The Packaging Gate chooses whether a title / thumbnail / opening-frame package
is approved before downstream research and script drafting.

Every candidate receives:

- ACCEPT;
- REWORK; or
- REJECT.

There is no numeric packaging score.

## Core rule

At most one package may be ACCEPTED per concept.

This prevents downstream research and scripting from inheriting multiple
competing promises.

## ACCEPT criteria

All configured checks must be true:

- promise_clear;
- concept_aligned;
- title_thumbnail_complementary;
- not_misleading;
- viewer_awareness_fit;
- payoff_defined;
- research_dependencies_explicit.

## Research handoff

An accepted package becomes part of the Research Engine handoff.

The handoff preserves the accepted concept and adds:

- package ID;
- title;
- thumbnail communication;
- opening-frame intent;
- expected viewer;
- awareness level;
- core promise;
- curiosity gap;
- expected payoff;
- title/thumbnail relationship;
- research dependencies;
- Packaging Gate provenance.

Those research dependencies become mandatory research questions in the Research
Engine.

## Boundary

Packaging approval means the human considers the promise worth researching and
accurate to the concept.

It does not mean every factual implication has already been verified.

Research still has to resolve the package dependencies before the Story /
Script Engine may use the approved promise.
