# Viewer Need, Gap & Fit Framing

## Purpose

This layer sharpens a proven topic into a video idea a specific viewer can
understand and care about.

A topic is not yet an idea.

~~~text
Topic
→ specific viewer problem
→ viewer moment
→ desired outcome
→ gap hypothesis
→ channel fit
→ three-title clarity test
→ packaging
→ one-sentence promise
~~~

## Required concept fields

### viewer_problem

The specific problem, question or curiosity the viewer is trying to resolve.

Bad:

~~~text
F1 brakes
~~~

Better:

~~~text
Why do F1 brakes need temperatures that would be undesirable for a road car?
~~~

### viewer_moment

The situation in which the viewer needs the answer.

Examples:

- trying to understand a surprising mechanism;
- comparing two engineering approaches;
- troubleshooting a performance problem;
- deciding what to buy or modify;
- trying to understand why a rule or design exists.

### desired_outcome

What the viewer wants to understand, fix, avoid or decide.

### content_gap

A content gap is never assumed from popularity alone.

Statuses:

- `SUPPORTED` — explicit evidence exists and is recorded;
- `HYPOTHESIS` — plausible gap to test;
- `UNASSESSED` — no defensible gap judgment yet.

Examples of possible gap hypotheses include:

- current videos are too broad;
- the mechanism is shown but not explained;
- examples are missing;
- explanations target the wrong knowledge level;
- an important follow-up question remains unanswered;
- current material appears outdated.

`SUPPORTED` requires a concrete evidence basis.

### channel_fit

Statuses:

- `FIT`
- `REVIEW`
- `UNASSESSED`

Channel fit asks whether the idea strengthens the audience/channel being built,
not merely whether the subject is popular.

### title_clarity_test

Before Concept Gate acceptance, provide at least three distinct working title
options.

The purpose is not to pick a winner. It tests whether the underlying idea is
specific enough to express clearly.

Results:

- `PASS`
- `REFRAME`

If three titles remain vague or generic, the idea should be reframed rather
than cosmetically rewritten.

## Packaging one-sentence test

Every package must include:

~~~text
This video helps [specific viewer/problem] so they can [specific outcome].
~~~

The package also records:

- viewer problem;
- viewer moment;
- desired outcome;
- gap positioning;
- channel-fit alignment.

## Human gates

Concept Gate checks:

- problem specificity;
- viewer moment clarity;
- outcome specificity;
- gap-evidence honesty;
- channel fit;
- title clarity.

Packaging Gate checks:

- problem alignment;
- moment fit;
- one-sentence promise clarity;
- honest gap positioning;
- preserved channel fit.

No numeric idea score, fit score or gap score is introduced.

## Comment mining — planned evidence source

Comment mining is not yet implemented.

When added, it should look for repeated:

- questions;
- requests for examples or follow-ups;
- points of confusion;
- steps where viewers remain stuck;
- narrower problems that existing videos did not resolve.

Repeated audience questions may support a content-gap hypothesis. A single
comment can suggest an idea but should not automatically be treated as
validated demand.

Until such evidence exists, the project must keep content-gap claims as
`HYPOTHESIS` or `UNASSESSED`.
